Two programs reach more Cook County residents than any others that H.R. 1 changes: Medicaid, which covers adults through the Affordable Care Act expansion, and SNAP, which helped about residents buy groceries in September 2025. When people lose either one, many turn to the county: its hospitals, its clinics, and its health plan.

The range above is wide on purpose. The middle of it depends on how people respond to new paperwork, and that is not known yet. The sections below show what has already happened and what each scenario assumes.
